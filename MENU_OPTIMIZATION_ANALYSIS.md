# 🔍 Menu Optimization Analysis

## Current vs. Recommended Menu Structure

### Current Issues:
1. **Inconsistent capitalization** (Resume vs The Vistas)
2. **Too specific** (individual community names should be sub-items)
3. **Unclear hierarchy** (Resume/Team separate from properties)
4. **Not action-oriented** for user experience

## 📋 **Specific Recommendations**

### **Priority Changes:**

1. **Resume** → **"Agent Profile"** or **"Meet Dr. Jan Duffy"**
   - ✅ More personal and welcoming
   - ✅ Better for SEO (name appears in URL)

2. **Team** → **"Our Team"**
   - ✅ Standard professional terminology
   - ✅ More inclusive language

3. **Properties** → Keep as is
   - ✅ Clear and action-oriented

4. **Communities** → **"Our Communities"** or **"Neighborhoods"**
   - ✅ More inviting language
   - ✅ Neighborhoods is more user-friendly

5. **Sub-divisions (San Marcos, etc.)** → **Nested under Communities**
   - ✅ Better information architecture
   - ✅ Cleaner main navigation
   - ✅ Expandable on hover/click

6. **The Vistas** → **Special category** or **Featured Community**
   - ✅ Can be a featured section
   - ✅ Or nested under Communities

7. **Press** → **"Press & News"** or **"Media"**
   - ✅ More professional
   - ✅ Clearer than "Press"

8. **Testimonials** → Keep or **"Client Success Stories"**
   - ✅ Social proof is important
   - ✅ "Stories" is more engaging
