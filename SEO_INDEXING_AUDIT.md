## Root Cause Analysis

### Issue: "Discovered - currently not indexed"
This typically happens when:
1. **Missing or insufficient content** - Pages with minimal content
2. **No follow directives** - robots blocking indexing
3. **Duplicate content** - Google sees duplicates
4. **Technical issues** - Loading/rendering problems
5. **Missing canonical URL** - No clear preferred URL
6. **Indexing restrictions** - Content policy violations
