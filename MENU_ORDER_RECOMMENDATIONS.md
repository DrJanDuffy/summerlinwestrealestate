### **Why This Order Works:**

**1. Properties First** ✅
- Most visitors come to find/buy/sell properties
- Primary call-to-action
- Industry standard for real estate sites

**2. Neighborhoods Second** ✅
- Critical for location-focused buyers
- Part of the search process
- Builds understanding of service area

**3. Success Stories Third** ✅
- Social proof early builds trust
- Addresses "Can I trust this agent?" question
- More credible when placed before bio

**4. Latest News Fourth** ✅
- Shows market expertise
- Builds credibility and knowledge
- Good for SEO (fresh content)

**5. Our Team Fifth** ✅
- Shows additional expertise/resources
- Less critical than individual bio
- Good for luxury market where team matters

**6. About Dr. Jan Duffy Sixth** ✅
- Personal connection isn't the first priority
- Most visitors don't need this immediately
- Important but not critical for initial visit

**7. Contact Last** ✅
- Natural progression - "now I'm ready to contact"
- Standard UX pattern
- Clear call-to-action
