# 🎯 Better Navigation Labels for Homeowners

## 💡 **Problem with "Resume"**

### **Why "Resume" Doesn't Work for Homeowners:**
❌ **Too corporate** - Sounds like a job application, not a real estate service
❌ **Not relatable** - Homeowners don't care about your resume, they care about results
❌ **Search intent mismatch** - People searching for realtors search for "agent", "realtor", "about"
❌ **Weak SEO** - "Resume" is not what people search for in real estate

---

## 🎯 **Better Options for Homeowners**

### **Top Recommendation: "About Dr. Jan Duffy"**
✅ **Personal & Professional** - Uses the agent's name
✅ **Homeowner-friendly** - Natural, conversational language
✅ **SEO Optimized** - "Dr. Jan Duffy" is a search term people use
✅ **Credibility** - Shows the agent is central to the business
✅ **Standard Practice** - Most real estate sites use "About [Name]"

### **Alternative Options:**

1. **"Meet Dr. Jan Duffy"** ✅
   - Warm, welcoming language
   - Emphasizes personal connection
   - Common on luxury real estate sites

2. **"Agent Profile"** ✅ 
   - Clear and professional
   - Homeowners understand what it means
   - Good for search

3. **"Our Story"** ✅
   - Warm and personal
   - Great for luxury brands
   - Emphasizes relationship

4. **"Why Dr. Jan Duffy"** ✅
   - Directly answers homeowner's question
   - Persuasive and conversion-focused
   - Great for SEO
